## Signs It Is Time to Book

You do not need to be a competitive athlete. Weekend runners, lifters, golfers and parents who train all qualify. Consider booking if:

- Pain is not improving after several days of rest
- The same injury keeps returning
- You are favoring one side or moving differently
- A joint feels stiff or will not move through its normal range
- It only hurts during training, even if it fades afterward

That last one still counts. Pain that shows up only under load often points to a movement problem worth fixing before it grows.

## Go to Urgent Care or the ER First If

Some injuries need medical care before any chiropractor. Get seen promptly if you have:

- A suspected fracture or dislocation, or you cannot bear weight
- A head injury or concussion signs
- Numbness or weakness that is getting worse
- Fever with pain, or loss of bladder or bowel control

If your exam shows something outside our scope, we will tell you plainly and point you to the right care.

### Shockwave Therapy: What the Evidence Supports

Shockwave therapy, also called acoustic wave therapy or ESWT, uses pressure waves on irritated tissue. Our clinic uses a STORZ MEDICAL device.

The evidence varies by condition. A [2023 systematic review and meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37662911/) found high-quality evidence of a large effect for plantar fasciitis. For patellar and Achilles tendinopathy, it found low-to-moderate evidence of a negligible effect, and [clinical guidelines conflict](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10468604/) on some uses. So we use it when it fits, not for everything. See [what shockwave therapy is used for](https://www.movemuscleandjoint.com/blog/what-is-shockwave-therapy-used-for).

### Rehab and Movement Retraining

This is where lasting results come from. Targeted exercises build strength and control in the pattern that failed, so the injury is less likely to return.

### Return to Training

Progress is judged by function: how you move, how much load you tolerate and whether pain stays quiet during your sport. We bring you back gradually.

## Frequently asked questions

### When should I see a chiropractor for a sports injury?

Consider it when pain is not improving with rest, keeps returning, limits your range of motion or changes how you move. Rule out red flags first, such as a suspected fracture or worsening numbness.

### Should I see a chiropractor or a physical therapist first?

Either can be a good start for many sports injuries. A chiropractor combining hands-on care with rehab, as we do, can cover both in one place. If your exam shows something outside our scope, we will refer you appropriately.

### Is shockwave therapy right for every sports injury?

No. Evidence is strongest for plantar fasciitis and weaker for patellar and Achilles tendinopathy. We use ESWT when it fits your problem, alongside movement-based care.
